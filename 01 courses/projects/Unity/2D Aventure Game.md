# 2D Aventure Game

### Genre definition

> *The Legend of Zelda* games feature a mix of puzzles, action, adventure/battle [gameplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gameplay), and exploration.
Later games in the series also include stealth gameplay, where the player must avoid enemies while proceeding through a level, as well as racing elements.

Every game in the main *Zelda* series has consisted of three principal areas: 
- an [overworld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overworld) which connects all other areas, in which movement is multidirectional, 
allowing the player some degree of freedom of action; 
- areas of interaction with other characters (merely caves or hidden rooms in the 
first game, but expanding to entire towns and cities in subsequent 
games) in which the player gains special items or advice, can purchase 
equipment or complete side quests;
- [dungeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_crawl) : areas of labyrinthine layout, usually underground, comprising a wide range of difficult enemies, [bosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_(video_gaming)), and items.

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda)
> 

### Features expected

- [ ]  Camera fixe, 2D (Pas de perspective)
- [ ]  2D movement
- [ ]  Open doors to access level : free doors/access, key locked doors/access, puzzle locked doors/access
- [ ]  Obstacles : Static obstacles, hazardous obstacles
- [ ]  Unlocking the end of the level

### Experience content

- [ ]  5 à 10 minutes de temps de jeu
- [ ]  5 à 10 salles
- [ ]  End game condition : Unlock the final level
