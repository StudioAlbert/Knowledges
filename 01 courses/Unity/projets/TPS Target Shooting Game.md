# TPS Target Shooting Game

## GamePlay reference

[https://youtu.be/ni_b6K9iJtM](https://youtu.be/ni_b6K9iJtM)

Create a game where the player can move around, shoot every targets in the zone before the timer finishes.

Create a first-person control for a character using a game engine. Implement basic abilities such as walking, running, jumping, looking around, etc..

Furthermore, all gameplay-relevant values for the control should be adjustable in the game engine without having to change the code directly.

### Features expected :

1. **Camera Perspective:** The player controls a character whose actions are viewed from a camera placed behind and slightly above or to the side of the character. This perspective allows players to see more of their character and the game world.
2. **Combat and Shooting Mechanics:** Shooting is a core element of TPS gameplay. Players aim and fire weapons at enemies while navigating the environment. The targeting reticle is often visible on the screen, aiding players in aiming at their targets.
3. **Character Movement:** TPS games typically involve fluid character movement. Players can traverse the environment, dodge attacks, and perform various maneuvers to gain a tactical advantage during combat.
4. Collecting targets via the shoot system : targets are fixed, in a limited number. They can be hidden in the environnement, but be sure the player could find and shoot all of them. 

### Experience content (Time / Levels)

1. Winning condition : Shoot all targets before time runs out
2. Loose condition : Time elapsed before all targets were shot 

### Optional features

1. **Cover System:** Many TPS games incorporate a cover system, where players can use objects or the environment as cover to protect themselves from enemy gunfire. Players can usually move between cover points strategically during combat.
2. **Storytelling and Narrative:** Many TPS games feature strong narratives and character-driven stories. Players often engage in missions or campaigns that advance a plot, and they may interact with non-playable characters (NPCs) to uncover the game's storyline.
3. **Multiplayer Modes:** TPS games often include multiplayer modes where players can compete against each other. These modes may involve team-based matches, free-for-all battles, or objective-based gameplay.
4. **Role-playing Elements:** Some TPS games incorporate role-playing elements, allowing players to customize their characters, upgrade weapons, and unlock new abilities as they progress through the game.

### Related Unity Skills

1. Character controller
2. New Input system
3. Animator
4. Shooting mechanics (Raycast)
5. Event Manager
6. External package knowledge : ProBuilder, Final IK

### Genre definition

> **Key features of Third Person Shooter games include:

Camera Perspective:** The player controls a character whose actions are viewed from a camera placed behind and slightly above or to the side of the character. This perspective allows players to see more of their character and the game world.

**Cover System:** Many TPS games incorporate a cover system, where players can use objects or the environment as cover to protect themselves from enemy gunfire. Players can usually move between cover points strategically during combat.

**Combat and Shooting Mechanics:** Shooting is a core element of TPS gameplay. Players aim and fire weapons at enemies while navigating the environment. The targeting reticle is often visible on the screen, aiding players in aiming at their targets.

**Character Movement:** TPS games typically involve fluid character movement. Players can traverse the environment, dodge attacks, and perform various maneuvers to gain a tactical advantage during combat.

**Storytelling and Narrative:** Many TPS games feature strong narratives and character-driven stories. Players often engage in missions or campaigns that advance a plot, and they may interact with non-playable characters (NPCs) to uncover the game's storyline.

**Multiplayer Modes:** TPS games often include multiplayer modes where players can compete against each other. These modes may involve team-based matches, free-for-all battles, or objective-based gameplay.

**Role-playing Elements:** Some TPS games incorporate role-playing elements, allowing players to customize their characters, upgrade weapons, and unlock new abilities as they progress through the game.

Popular examples of Third Person Shooter games include the Gears of War series, The Division series, Max Payne, Tomb Raider, and Uncharted. The genre has evolved over time, incorporating elements from other genres and often pushing the boundaries of graphical and gameplay innovation.

> The Third Person Shooter (TPS) is a genre of video games that involves gameplay from a third-person perspective, where the player views the action from a camera angle situated behind the playable character. In contrast to first-person shooters (FPS), where the player sees the game world through the eyes of the character, TPS provides a wider view of the character and the surrounding environment.
