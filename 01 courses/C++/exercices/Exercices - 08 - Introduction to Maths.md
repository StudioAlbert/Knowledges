## Exercise 10 — Smash Plast Banic

Some of the best players of Smash Plast Banic played several rounds. Here are some of the statistics of the game.

**Round 1**

| Players | Kills | Outs | Results |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Luca | 4 | 5 | -1 |
| Philippe | 6 | 3 | 3 |
| Guy | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Dyrapide | 6 | 7 | -1 |

**Round 2**

| Players | Kills | Outs | Results |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Luca | 7 | 6 | 1 |
| Philippe | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| Guy | 6 | 7 | -1 |
| Dyrapide | 2 | 4 | -2 |

**Round 3**

| Players | Kills | Outs | Results |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Luca | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Philippe | 8 | 2 | 6 |
| Guy | 1 | 7 | -6 |
| Dyrapide | 5 | 5 | 0 |

What are the expected values of kills and outs? What are the standard deviations of the first round, the second round and the third round separately?

Draw a graph with the progression of everyone's results!
